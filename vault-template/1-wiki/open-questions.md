# Open Questions

Contradictions the compiler refused to resolve on its own. Each entry names
the conflicting sources so it can be settled against ground truth rather than
by whoever edited last.

Format: `- **[claim]** — source A says X, source B says Y. Pages: [[...]]`

**No other knowledge tool has this view.** It is a live list of what you — or
your team — disagree with yourselves about.
