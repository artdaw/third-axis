# Contested Example

When a new source disagrees with what a page already says, the page is not
overwritten. **Both positions stay**, the confidence becomes `contested`, and
the conflict is logged in `1-wiki/open-questions.md`.

Today, in every normal wiki, the last person to edit a page wins — silently,
and without anyone knowing a disagreement ever existed.
